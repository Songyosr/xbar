# Design Philosophy: Making Stats Visual

Creating educational tools requires balancing functionality with simplicity. Here's how we approach designing our statistical applets.

## Less is More

Our interfaces focus on the essential controls. Every slider, button, and visualization serves a specific learning purpose. We ask: "Does this help or hinder understanding?"

## Color with Purpose

- **Navy blue** for primary elements and data
- **Orange** for highlights and interactive elements  
- **Subtle grays** for supporting information
- High contrast for accessibility

## Motion Matters

Animations aren't just decoration—they show *process*. Watching sample means accumulate helps build intuition about the Central Limit Theorem in ways static images can't.

## Mobile-First Thinking

Statistics learning happens everywhere. Our applets work on phones, tablets, and desktops. Touch-friendly controls and responsive layouts ensure everyone can explore.

## What's Next

We're constantly refining based on user feedback. Small improvements in clarity can make huge differences in learning outcomes.

*"The best interface is the one that gets out of the way of learning."*

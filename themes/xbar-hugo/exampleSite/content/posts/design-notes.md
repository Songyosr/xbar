Color, spacing, and motion choices for clarity and calm.

## Color Palette

The XBAR color system uses a minimal palette designed for high contrast and accessibility:

- **Ink (#001524)**: Primary text color, ensuring maximum readability
- **Navy (#15616D)**: Primary accent for parameter lines and UI elements
- **Orange (#FF7D00)**: Call-to-action color and active states
- **Info (#475569)**: Secondary text for metadata and descriptions

## Typography

Inter for body text provides excellent readability at all sizes, while JetBrains Mono adds subtle coding references without overwhelming the design.

## Motion

Animations are kept minimal and purposeful:
- Subtle hover states with 1px translateY
- Blinking cursor at 1.15s intervals
- Teaching animations run 800-1200ms for optimal comprehension

## The "Code Bar" Aesthetic

Colored brackets `[` and `]` in navy and orange provide visual anchors without being overly technical. The design walks the line between academic clarity and modern web aesthetics.

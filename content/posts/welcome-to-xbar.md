# Welcome to XBAR

Welcome to our collection of interactive statistics applets! We're excited to share these tools that make statistical concepts more intuitive and engaging.

## Our Philosophy

Statistics can be abstract and intimidating. We believe that **interactive visualization** helps bridge the gap between theory and understanding. Instead of just reading about the Central Limit Theorem, you can *see* it in action.

## What's Coming

We're constantly adding new applets and improving existing ones. Upcoming features include:

- More distribution visualizations
- Regression analysis tools
- Bayesian statistics explorers
- Custom data upload capabilities

## Get Involved

Found a bug? Have a suggestion? We'd love to hear from you! Check out our GitHub repository or reach out through our contact page.

Happy exploring! 📊

A short on the name, teaching goals, and this tiny lab.

## The Name

x̄ (x-bar) represents the sample mean in statistics — the average of a sample drawn from a population. It's one of the most fundamental concepts in statistical inference, bridging the gap between what we observe and what we can infer about the world.

## Teaching Goals

This project aims to make statistical concepts more intuitive through interactive visualization. Rather than just showing formulas, we want students to *see* and *feel* how sampling distributions emerge, how confidence intervals behave, and why p-values work the way they do.

## The Lab

Built with modern web technologies but designed to be embeddable anywhere — from slides to Bookdown documents. No complex build steps, just clean HTML, CSS, and JavaScript that works everywhere.

The "Code Bar" aesthetic reflects the intersection of statistics and programming, acknowledging that modern data analysis happens at the command line as much as in textbooks.
